# DECISIONS: what changed from v1 to v2, and why

Evidence IDs: **B1** market and pricing review, **B2** product and ops review, **B3** CFO and risk review (files in founder/bolo-review/), **PANEL** the 17-person simulated panel (simulated, an upper bound), **MODEL** the 12-month cohort model in BUSINESS_PLAN.md section 10, **QA** the independent check in qa.md (its fixes are applied in v2.1). All web sources were snippet-only.

| 1      | Monthly PKR 1,499 as the hero                     | Pro 30 days PKR 799, test 999 on 30%; 1,499 only as Plus in Phase 3                                                                | B1: 3.7% of a PKR 40,000 minimum wage; SpeakX realises about PKR 875. PANEL: 0 of 12 would pay upfront                                                                                                           |

| 30     | In-app browser check at sign-in; no PWA prompt    | Check on S01 before the test; PWA install prompt, renewal card and day-28 offer on S07                                             | QA; B2                                                                                                                                                            |

**Kept from v1:** PWA on Next.js and Supabase; Urdu explanations; HR-written question bank; minutes and day caps on the server; WhatsApp only for onboarding, reminders and confirmations; audio deleted after 30 days; phase gates with stop rules; free voice level test as the hook.

**Not changed because the Board had no evidence either way:** Maqsad and Asan English prices (unknown); Gulf blue-collar demand for English (unknown); AI cost per minute (v1 planning number, measure in Phase 0); wallet SMS formats for the matcher (verify with real samples); gateway eligibility for a sole proprietor (verify).
